# 37 mm drive-motor mount measurement gauge

`37mm-mount-hole-gauge.scad` is a low-cost confirmation coupon for the four
purchased Grobotronics `19-00037912` steel mounts. It is not a structural
motor mount.

Provisional hand measurements recorded on 2026-10-09:

- mounting foot: approximately 40 x 40 mm;
- hole-centre rectangle: 25 mm longitudinal x 30 mm transverse;
- first longitudinal row: 9 mm from the motor-side upright/edge;
- hole diameter: approximately 4 mm;
- bracket/motor height: approximately 47 mm (not represented by this flat
  gauge and still requiring a precise datum definition).

Print the coupon flat at 100% scale. Put its notched `MOTOR` edge against the
motor-side upright and insert the real fasteners through all four holes. Do not
force the screws. Record corrected measurements if any hole fails to align.
Only the confirmed pattern may be used for the structural PLA drive nodes.

## Physical fit checkpoint — 2026-10-09

The 2 mm PLA coupon was installed on one real steel mount with all four
fasteners. The photographed fit shows the 40 x 40 mm outline, motor-side edge
orientation and four-hole pattern aligning without visible forced distortion.
This promotes the pattern to **physically fit-checked on one sample**, not yet
lot-verified across all four mounts. Hole diameter, exact bracket height/axle
datum and repeatability across the remaining three mounts still require direct
measurement before structural drive-node release.
