# Dupont 3+3 front-height fit check

The photographs of the printed shell show a visible gap between the two
halves at the driver-side nose with three individual female jumper housings
in each row. The v2 CAD assumed a packed-block height of **5.48 mm**,
including 0.40 mm total clearance. The photographs do not give a reliable
numeric gap, so the correct replacement dimension cannot be inferred from
them alone.

Three small U-shaped coupons test larger internal heights of **6.0, 6.5 and
7.0 mm**. Print **two copies of one size**, starting with 6.0 mm. With the
driver disconnected from all power, lay one coupon against each row of the
six female jumper housings (rotate one coupon 180° in-plane). They should
meet without squeezing the plastic ends. The coupon uses an intentionally
generous 12.0 mm x opening, so this test isolates the top-to-bottom height;
it does not certify the v2 x fit, retention, pin alignment or board clearance.

The corresponding external heights are **7.8, 8.1 and 8.6 mm**. Check the
same coupon against the real driver's nearby components. If the first size
that accepts the jumpers cannot also clear the driver, simply widening the
v2 shell is not a safe solution. The next design would need a different
contact-retention approach (for example removing the individual Dupont
plastic housings and using a purpose-made keyed connector).

Do not tighten the M3 bolts of the gapped v2 print to pull its front closed.
