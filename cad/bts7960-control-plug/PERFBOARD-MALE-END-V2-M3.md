# M3-clamped perfboard cable plugs — fit-test revision

The 2-pin and 4-pin plugs are each printed as a lower and upper half around
individual **male Dupont jumper ends**. Their keyed front nose, 2.54 mm pin
spacing, and cable-end pocket are unchanged from the v1 prototype. The rear
is now held with **two M3 through-screws and nuts per plug**, instead of a
cable tie. The two screw axes run across the split between halves, outside
the cable exit. Their printed clearance holes are 3.25 mm.

Print one 2-pin pair and one 4-pin pair before making all four driver sets.
Lay the four or two jumper ends in the lower half, place the upper half,
insert both M3 screws, and tighten the nuts only enough to close the seam.
The printed lugs are small: do not use large washers or overtighten. Select
screw length against the printed 8 mm body plus the actual nuts; M3×12 mm is
a starting fit candidate, not a guaranteed hardware specification.

Check that the purchased jumper housings are gripped without crushing their
metal contacts, that the metal pins protrude enough to seat fully in the
female perfboard header, and that the unplugged plug stays closed. Then test
both plugs together in the v2 cover; their nominal rear-body gap at one
2+4 driver pair is about 3.4 mm before screw heads/nuts or print variation.
No motor power or 12 V runs through these logic connectors.
