# BTS7960 jumper holder v3 — extra front clearance

This is a direct fit revision of v2 based on the photographed gap between the
two printed halves with three individual female jumper ends in each row.

- Front outside height (`nose_y`) increases from **7.8 to 9.8 mm**: +1.0 mm
  on the outside of each half.
- Internal packed-end pocket height increases from **5.48 to 7.48 mm**: +1.0
  mm available inside each half.
- The eight mating-hole centres remain at the original **2.54 mm row pitch**
  and 2.54 mm column pitch. They appear 1 mm closer to the centre split
  *relative to each enlarged outer edge*, but have not moved relative to the
  driver's pins.
- Nose length, M3 holes and cable routing stay as in v2. The two IS dummies
  are unchanged.

Print one lower and one upper half only for the first dry fit; reuse two v2
dummy pieces if available. Do not force the female housings into place or use
the bolts to close a remaining gap. Check that the larger 9.8 mm nose clears
the real driver's nearby components. Because the pocket is more generous,
also check that the six jumper housings do not rattle or shift relative to the
front apertures. If either check fails, this is not a usable final connector.

The fixed 2×4 pin grid remains 180° symmetric. `VCC` is a visual mark, not a
mechanical reverse-insertion key. Confirm all pin assignments and isolation
of the IS positions with a meter before applying power.
