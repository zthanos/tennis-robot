# Rear-pod archive — ΜΗΝ ΤΥΠΩΣΕΙΣ

Recoverable historical rear-pod material. Active release:
`../rear-pod-v2.scad`, `../params-v2.scad` and `../stl-v2/` as listed in
`../../ACTIVE-PRINT-SET.md`.

- `stl-v0/`: P2-72 prototype CAD/STL and review images; replaced by P2-75.
- `stl-v1/`: P2-75 stackable-shelf CAD/STL and export script; replaced by
  the upright carrier P2-76 and strengthened P2-79/P2-81 release.
- `p276-inactive-assets/`: old rear-pod MPU mount and chassis spacers. The
  active MPU is the P2-77 rail-side mount; the P2-79 saddle needs no spacers.
- `p277-service-proposal/`: review-only proposal replaced by P2-79/P2-81.
- `review-images/`: unreferenced review renders.

Do not mix any archived STL with the P2-81 rear pod.
